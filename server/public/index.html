Quiet mode

iOS plays a chime whenever it starts listening, and no app can silence it. Keeping the mic ready means it chimes once when you start instead of on every sentence. The trade: the mic stays open during pauses, and iOS sends that audio to Apple to transcribe. Turn this off and you get a chime per sentence but the mic closes immediately.

Add Sotto to your home screen
  1. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom centre of Safari.
  2. Swipe up on the list that appears.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, top right.
  5. Leave Safari and open Sotto from your home screen.

It opens fullscreen and already paired, so it is one tap to talk.
