Speak into your phone.
It types on your laptop.

Prompt Claude or ChatGPT by voice, at a volume only you can hear. Hold a button on your phone and the words land in your laptop’s chat box.

Add to Chrome — free

Then scan a QR with your phone. About two minutes.

How it works

  1. Install the Chrome extension on your laptop
  2. Scan the QR code that appears, with your phone camera
  3. Hold the button on your phone and speak quietly
  4. The words appear in your AI chat box as you say them

Where it works

claude.ai, chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, aistudio.google.com and perplexity.ai.

Encrypted end to end

Your speech is turned into text on your phone and encrypted before it leaves the device. The relay forwards text it has no key to read — not as a policy, but by construction. Your phone and laptop agree on a key directly, and both show a matching safety number so you can check nobody intercepted the exchange.

Two things worth knowing. iPhone plays a short chime when it starts listening — Apple gives no way to mute it, so it fires once per session rather than once per sentence. And the phone app needs Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android, because that is where on-device speech recognition lives.
